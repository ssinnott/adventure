# Meridian Camp's decisions

Meridian Camp's decisions (EXPANSION §8.2): the old §8 of its doc, `docs/areas/meridian_camp.md`,
moved here whole by #1027, its five lists in their order. It is appended to and never rewritten:
a builder adds a list at its end, and a later decision that changes an earlier one says which.
