**pitfalls:**
- Circular union types: deriving a union from the defs' own fields is circular. Examples: (typeof MONSTERS)[number]['sprite'], or HARROW's feature interiors, while those tables and maps are typed against MonsterDef or MapDef. I reproduced this with the repo's tsc 5.9: TS7022, TS2456 and TS2502. Derive the unions from standalone literal registries instead: an `as const` kinds or interiors object or tuple, or the keys of the AREAS object. A registry that namespace-imports each area, with `satisfies` on the tables, typechecked and still rejected a bad sprite.
- Unions that silently become `string`: `keyof typeof` today's tables gives `string`, because MONSTERS, ITEMS and SPELLS are Record<string, …> built with Object.fromEntries (monsters.ts:46, items.ts:36, spells.ts:33). I verified that such a union accepts any string with no error. FAMILY (sprites.ts:152) and SCENES (interior.ts:17) would then stop catching a missing drawing or room.
