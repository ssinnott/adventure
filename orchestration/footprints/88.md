**pitfalls:**

1. `giftOf` and `spentId` end in `default: return undefined` (70ff009 `src/game/wilds.ts:21`,
   `:30`). A den left out of them still compiles. Its hoard then escapes the curve and the window,
   its id escapes the shipped list, and its brood's `until` fails `condFaults` as naming nothing.
   The typecheck flags only `interact`'s `never` (70ff009 `game.ts:200`) and `featureLabel` (`:333`)
   (tried).
2. A burnt den's id is its spent id, so #45's automap skip (70ff009 `src/ui/frame.ts:141-142`) hides
   it. The ruin must be drawn before that line.
3. `kind` is taken twice: by the union's discriminant (`src/game/map.ts:61-73`) and by
   `MonsterDef.kind`, beast to machine (`src/game/monsters.ts:11`, `:34`). The den's monster is
   `breeds`, a list of def ids (the bandit camp breeds bandits and their archers).
4. **Keepers on the den's square.** Space facing it would act on the den, since a feature ahead wins
   over a group (`game.ts:260-268`), while the hint line offers the fight (`game.ts:305`). Keep them
   beside it, as the Grove's guards stand by their chests (`grove1.ts:41`/`:56`,
   `grove2.ts:49`/`:64`). A step into the den then starts their fight, and the won fight's
   `enterCell` (`game.ts:210`) puts the choice.
5. `until` needs `respawn`: a group with `until` and no `respawn` is refused (`structure.ts:90`). So
   keepers carry neither, and every brood group needs `respawn`, both for that and for
   `liveGroups`' return (`world.ts:380`). The pace is the brood's `respawn`, not a field of the den:
   one number, held to 720-2,880 (`structure.ts:17`).
6. Keepers with `when` or `after` would leave the den unguarded yet unburnable: they are absent but
   alive, and `keepersDead` reads `dead`. Refuse them.
7. `layOutdoors` moves a feature's own x and y only (`src/game/outdoors.ts:79`, `:96`). Name the
   keepers and brood by id: a square held inside the den would stay in zone coordinates.
8. **The pace.**
   - It needs no saved field, only a waiting brood group's `dead` moved on to the start of its turn.
   - A plain sort on `dead` without moving it brings all back a pace after the first (tried).
   - Sorting the waiting by their kept minute keeps one a pace, but not strictly their order of
     death. That is harmless, since the order is not promised.
   - It runs lazily with `liveGroups`: a company away five days finds all three back (tried), as it
     would any respawn.

9. `liveGroups` is hot: it runs for every drawn cell each frame (`game.ts:236-241`) and again for the

11. The log is four lines (`frame.ts:204`). A den's look (two lines) and its keepers' kind's look

12. `seen` refs are split on ':' (`quests.ts:74`). The look's key uses '.', never ':'.

**open_decisions** (each a default a session can take alone):

1. **"They roam from the den."** Default: a brood group comes back where it was placed, its post in
   the box, as every group does (`world.ts:380`), so the den keeps the box stocked. Coming back at
   the den would leave it there, since a group moves only towards a company within its `aware`
   (`world.ts:400-417`), and the brood would gather round the den. A group that walks out to a post
   is a movement system the game lacks.
2. **"Drawn as a ruin."** Default: the automap's mark (a colour of the den's own, then ash) and a
   line on stepping in (`ruin`, optional). Nothing is drawn in the view for any feature today (#115's
   choice 7). A ruin in the view would be new art. The camp's own squares changing would be new saved
   state, cells by coordinate as doors are.
3. **One a pace**, the issue's "one each day that fewer are abroad", rather than each brood group
   on its own timer. The timer is simpler, but three killed together come back together. Default:
   one a pace.
4. **Its flag is its id in `used`,** read by `seen`, not a party flag (above). A quest can still
   read it: `seen: 'e2:e2_rookery'`.
5. **The keepers stand beside the den,** not on it (pitfall 4). They never roam and never come back,
   and carry no `when` or `after`.
6. **Its kind is `breeds`** (def ids), and every brood group is made of them only. The keepers are
   not held to it: the rookery's are "the old birds".
7. **The number:** no cap ("a den may set its own number"). The gate's reading prints it.
8. **The hoard** is given at the burning, in one line after it ("The hoard: 40 gold, Spear +1."),
   not left as a chest to open.
9. **The choice** is put on a step and on Space, through `ChoiceScreen` (or #76's `Game.ask` if it
   is in first; see collides_with). "Leave it" leaves the den standing, and it asks again next time.
   Esc is "Leave it".
10. **The look** is said the first time the den's square is in sight, by #41's sight rule, and kept
    in `used` (default). The other reading, on the first step beside it, needs no wait on #41, but
    needs a hook of its own and comes a step later.
11. **The gate's reading:** the keepers are the camp's hardest fight. No threshold of its own beyond
    the map's.
12. **The label** is its `name`, or 'A den', unchanged when burnt: the ruin shows on the automap and
    in its line.
