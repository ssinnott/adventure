**pitfalls:**
- shot.ts, gallery.ts, interiors.ts and worldmap.ts are scripts with top-level await and no exports (e.g. shot.ts:8-54); importing one runs it. 'Built from' means porting their in-page code, or first refactoring them into modules, which touches files other PRs edit.
- Each tool starts its own server and Chromium: shot about 1.7 s, gallery 1.6 s, interiors 1.3 s, worldmap 4.6 s (renderCloth paints the whole 2080x1568 cloth). The sheet should do everything in one page.
- Every run is a new game seeded from Math.random (freshSeed, src/ui/title.ts:27), so the weather differs run to run. Pin it the way shot.ts does (world.state.weatherSeed at shot.ts:31, w.sky = null at :40), and pin the date too: trees change with the season, and a new game starts on 1 Mistfall.
- A new game's automap shows only what has been seen (World.reveal, src/game/world.ts:317). Mark every cell explored before capturing. On the outdoors the automap shows only a window round the party (src/ui/frame.ts:103-112), not the whole zone.
- 'Landmark' is not in the data; MapDef has no such field. Atlas sites (src/content/atlas.ts:311-324) give outdoor landmarks in zone-local coordinates: Thornhold 23,3.2; Old Tower 4.8,5; Barrow 27.8,8.4; The Grove 7.5,28.6. Towns and dungeons have none. Either derive landmarks from features (npc, rift, well, event, business doors) or add a field, which is a MapDef change in lane: systems and collides with #29. Sites are points, so the camera needs a standing square and a facing chosen next to each.
- 'Arrival' should be def.start plus every other map's exit into the map. Thornmark has three: 1,9 facing east from the Shelf pass (flag-gated, though travel() bypasses it), 23,5 from Thornhold, and 7,28 from grove1. Thornhold's is 7,14 facing north.
- World-map crop: cloth px = 16 + x*4, from S = 4 (worldmap.ts:25) and PAD = 16 (:27), neither exported. The Thornmark zone, at atlas [232,30], is cloth 944,136, 128x128; the Shelf, at [200,30], is 816,136. For a town or dungeon, find its outdoor map with homeMap() (src/game/atlas.ts:768) and crop round its exit square.
- Area scope today: only Thornmark's maps set region: 'thornmark' (thornmark.ts:13, thornhold.ts:12, grove1.ts:11, grove2.ts:12). Their encounters place 16 monster ids, 13 of them new: dire_wolf, ogre, brigand, brigand_archer, bone_knight, wraith, thorn_spider, rift_hound, zealot, ashen_adept, riftling_elder, ashen_hand, cut_warden (boar, riftling and skeleton are the Shelf's). Thornhold's interiors, from its features: green_man, lantern_chapterhouse, thornhold_armoury, lantern_hall, elders_yard, split_oak.
- gallery --only filters by sprite kind, not monster id (gallery.ts:67). A strip at --scale 2 with 6 frames is 1940 px wide per monster (gallery.ts:80), so 13 strips come to about 1940x5900 px. Keep the whole sheet small enough to attach and view.
- interiors.ts uses its own daylight curve (interiors.ts:105), not calendar.daylightAt. 'Nine at night' is --hour 21, daylight 0. Dungeons have no day and night: paintScene darkens only when the map is not a dungeon (viewport.ts:206).
- The in-page module URLs are invisible to the typechecker and break silently at #29.
- shot.ts finds Playwright and Chromium differently from the others (shot.ts:9 and :14: no /usr/lib path, no PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM). Copy smoke.ts:14-27. On the runner, Playwright is whatever #27 installs.

That satisfies 'a pull request's checks carry its sheet', but the image is not shown inline. Job summaries cannot show data: URIs.
