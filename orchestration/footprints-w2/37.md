**open_decisions:**
- How the sheet reaches the PR. Default: an artifact on the run, with a link in the job summary, and no write permissions. An inline image in a PR comment, hosted on a branch, needs contents: write and pull-requests: write and is the owner's call.
- What a landmark is. Default: for outdoor zones, the atlas sites (planned ones skipped), shot from the nearest open square two to three back, facing the site. Towns and dungeons get their arrivals plus the full automap. No new MapDef or Area field.
- What --area thornmark covers. Default: all four maps (outdoors, Thornhold, grove1, grove2), which is more than the Done-when names. The monsters are the area's 13 defs, and the interiors are its 6.
- What counts as new in CI. Default: maps whose files changed, or new ids; monsters that are new or changed against the base registry, which is loaded in Node from `git archive <base> src`, plus defs drawn by a changed family module (by its KINDS). Interiors are those whose scene file changed, or new ids. A change to a shared art helper (common.ts, gloss.ts, sprites.ts, interiors/kit.ts and the rest) includes all of that kind. Renderer-only changes add no maps automatically; the session passes --area.
- Format. Default: one PNG, composed in the page, with each stage shot cropped to viewport plus automap.
- Dungeons. Default: one shot with Light on and no night shot, since they have no daylight.
- Date and weather. Default: seed pinned as in smoke (1); a fixed high-summer day, so trees are in leaf; a clear sky; noon, and the first hour of full dark.
- Shared browser helper. Default: none. Copy smoke's plumbing, and refactor no existing tool in this PR.
- Required check. Default: the sheet job is not required, and the owner may add it to branch protection.
